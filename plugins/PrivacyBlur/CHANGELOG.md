# Privacy Blur changelog

Newest first.

## 1.5 (2026-11-16)

- A floating **Blur is on / Blur is off** button on Settings pages (only there), to flip blur without
  finding Privacy Blur in the plugin list; handy on a phone, where there's no Shift+Alt+B.
- Marker names are covered too: with **Replace instead of blurring** they keep their time and get the
  scene's stand-in title ("7:44 - Morning Light") on the Markers page, a scene's Markers tab and their
  tooltips; with blurring, they're blurred. The name box of a new marker and file names (which can't be
  swapped) are blurred in replace mode until you tap in them.

## 1.4 (2026-11-16)

- **Replace instead of blurring**: safe drawn stand-in pictures instead of a blur, and made-up performer and studio names, titles, gallery names, tags and details (with the **Also hide** switches).
- **Also hide tags** (blurs or replaces tag names).
- Replace mode also swaps studio codes for made-up ones.

## 1.3 (2026-11-09)

- Also blurs the rows from [Stash Suggestions](../StashSuggestions) (pictures, names and titles).

## 1.2 (2026-11-05)

- First release in this repository.
